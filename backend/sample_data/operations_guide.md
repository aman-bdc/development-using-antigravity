# Global Corporate Operations & Policy Manual (2024-2025)

## 1. Executive Summary and Company Mission
Our mission is to empower distributed teams with secure cloud infrastructure and intelligent data analytics. As of fiscal year 2024, our operations span four primary territories: North America, Europe, Asia Pacific, and Latin America. This guide standardizes cross-border communication, security policies, remote work, customer support SLA tiers, and expense approvals.

## 2. Information Security and Data Compliance
All customer and enterprise data must be processed following Zero Trust architecture:
- **Data Encryption**: All data at rest must be encrypted using AES-256-GCM. All data in transit must utilize TLS 1.3 or higher.
- **Access Control**: Role-based access control (RBAC) with mandatory multi-factor authentication (MFA) via FIDO2 hardware keys or authenticator applications. SMS-based 2FA is deprecated and disallowed.
- **Incident Response Time**: High-severity security events (Severity 1) must be acknowledged within 15 minutes and contained within 2 hours. A post-mortem incident report must be completed and delivered to the VP of Engineering within 48 business hours.

## 3. Remote Work & Flexible Workplace Guidelines
- **Core Collaboration Hours**: Distributed team members must remain synchronously reachable during regional core collaboration hours (10:00 AM to 3:00 PM local time).
- **Home Office Stipend**: Every full-time employee is eligible for an initial $1,500 home office setup stipend upon hiring, along with a recurring $75 monthly wellness and connectivity reimbursement.
- **Workation Policy**: Employees may work from any approved international location for up to 30 consecutive calendar days per calendar year, subject to manager pre-approval and local tax treaty review.

## 4. Customer Support Tiers & Service Level Agreements (SLAs)
We provide three standard service tiers:
1. **Tier 1 (Enterprise Dedicated)**:
   - 24/7/365 telephone, Slack, and video support.
   - Guaranteed initial response time under 15 minutes for critical outages.
   - 99.99% monthly platform uptime guarantee with service credit penalties.
2. **Tier 2 (Business Pro)**:
   - 16/5 business hours priority ticketing and live chat.
   - Guaranteed response within 1 hour for high-priority incidents.
   - 99.9% uptime guarantee.
3. **Tier 3 (Standard Starter)**:
   - Asynchronous email and ticketing support.
   - Response within 8 business hours.
   - Access to community knowledge base and developer documentation.

## 5. Travel & Expense Reimbursement Policy
- **Air Travel**: Economy class for flights under 6 hours; Premium Economy or Business Class authorized for flights exceeding 6 hours with VP approval.
- **Lodging**: Maximum daily hotel allowance of $250 in standard metropolitan areas and $350 in high-cost tier cities (e.g., New York, London, Tokyo, San Francisco).
- **Per Diem Meals**: $85 daily cap for all meals and incidental travel expenses. Receipts are required for all individual expenses exceeding $25.
